    Metal Extraction Quest II

    S3 chemistry adventure: mine ores, run a real blast furnace, electrolyse aluminium, craft weapons and defeat the Ore Beast. Every station follows the Lesson 18 chemistry.

    How metals are extracted (remember this!)

    • A metal's position in the reactivity series decides the method.
    • Below carbon (Fe, Cu…) → heat the ore with carbon / carbon monoxide.
    • Above carbon (Al…) → only electrolysis (electricity) works.
    • Very unreactive (Ag, Hg) → oxides break down on heating alone; Au is found free.
