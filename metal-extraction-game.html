    Metal Extraction Quest

    S3 chemistry adventure: wood → carbon → metal. Mine, burn charcoal, extract iron & copper, craft a weapon, defeat the Ore Beast!

    How to extract metals (remember this!)

    • 1. Chop wood in the south forest
    • 2. Kiln: wood → carbon (think prompts once, then free to remake)
    • 3. Furnace G1: iron ore + carbon → iron; heat carbon alone → upgrade to G2 + electricity
    • 4. Furnace G2: aluminium ore + electricity → Al metal → craft armour
    • 5. Craft a weapon to unlock the boss · Al armour recommended (no armour = one-hit death)
    • Wrong answers add +10 s to your mission timer (top-right)

    Metals look different

    • Iron ore — rusty brown stripes (west) → needs carbon extraction
    • Copper ore — green spots (east) → needs carbon extraction
    • Aluminium ore — pale grey-white (scarce) — needs Grade 2 furnace + electricity
    • Silver oxide (Ag₂O) — rare corners; heat → silver metal (not free metal)
    • Gold — free pure metal (rare corner); craft weapons directly
    • Electricity is shown as ⚡ (thunder)

    Chemistry recap (S3)

    • Wood → carbon: burn wood in a kiln to make charcoal (carbon).
    • Metal extraction needs two substances: ore + carbon.
    • Iron: iron ore + carbon → iron metal
    • Copper: copper ore + carbon → copper metal
    • Carbon helps remove oxygen from the ore so pure metal is left.
    • Mining digs ores up; extraction turns compounds into pure metal.
    • Gold = free metal; silver oxide must be heated: 2Ag₂O → 4Ag + O₂.
    • Different metals, different methods: Fe/Cu + carbon; Ag₂O heat; Al + ⚡.
    • Word equations + balanced equations are tested in the mission.
    • Al armour changes your look and stops one-hit death. Timer: wrong answer +10 s.

    Defeated!

    Without aluminium armour, one wrong answer defeats you. Craft Al armour (G2 furnace → Al metal → anvil) and try again. Inventory is kept. Timer keeps running.
