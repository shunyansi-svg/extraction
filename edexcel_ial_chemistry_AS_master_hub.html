PEF Reminders - Principal Examiner Feedback (2019-2025) from chemistry-wiki wchNN-examiner-insights
  • Definitions must be learnt word-for-word: relative atomic mass = 'mean/average mass of an atom relative to 1/12 of the mass of a carbon-12 atom' (BOTH parts needed, Jun 2023/2024); 1st IE = 'one mole of electrons from one mole of GASEOUS atoms -> one mole of gaseous 1+ ions' (state symbols crucial, Jan 2020).
  • Mass spectrometry: ionisation = electron bombardment (NOT electron affinity); accelerator = electric field, deflector = magnetic field; molecular ion M+ = Mr; ALWAYS write the charge on the ion; deflection depends on mass, NOT abundance.
  • Cl2 mass spectrum: THREE peaks m/z 70/72/74 in 9:6:1 (include the mixed-isotope 35Cl37Cl+ peak); Br2 peaks 78/80 = 75%:25%; ClF3+ peaks 92/94 ~ 3:1.
  • Cu2+ config: Cu2+ removes one electron from 4s AND one from 3d (Cu2+ = [Ar]3d9), not both from d; Cl = 1s2 2s2 2p6 3s2 3p5 (don't add 3d/4s).
  • Shapes: BeCl2 linear 180 (covalent); BF3 trigonal planar 120 (no lone pairs); PCl5 trigonal bipyramidal 90+120; water 104.5 (not 107); SF6 octahedral non-polar; Al2Cl6 dative bond Cl->Al (lone pair on Cl), no Al-Al bond.
  • Metallic conductivity: 'delocalised electrons that can move/flow' + electrostatic attraction; ionic conductivity: mobile IONS in liquid, not delocalised electrons; solid doesn't conduct.
  • Balancing + state symbols always: MgCl2(aq) not (s); balance charge AND atoms in ionic equations; learn ion charges (Mg2+, Cl-).
  • Free-radical substitution: initiation (UV, Cl2->2Cl, homolytic, fish-hook arrows), propagation, termination = TWO radicals combine.
  • Round only the FINAL answer to the stated sig figs (4 d.p. is not 4 s.f.); atom economy denominator = total molar mass of PRODUCTS; ideal gas: volume in m3, temperature in Kelvin (+273).
  • Observation vs inference: 'bubbles/effervescence' is the observation; 'hydrogen gas evolved' is an inference; nitrogen gas is a molecule (N2).
